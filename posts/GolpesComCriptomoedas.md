The cryptocurrency market has gained strong momentum in 2025, with more and more people entering the world of digital assets. But as investments increase, so do scams and cyberattacks.

According to data from ABFintechs, **losses from crypto-related fraud grew 37% in the last year alone**.

### The most common digital scams

Experts highlight three types of fraud as the most frequent today:

- **Phishing** - Criminals create fake websites and apps to steal passwords and digital wallets. 
- **Financial pyramid schemes** - Scams that promise impossible profits, using cryptocurrencies as a façade. 
- **Fake exchanges** - Platforms that allow deposits but block withdrawals once users invest.
> “There is no guaranteed return in the financial market — when the promise is too good, it’s a scam,” warns cybersecurity analyst **Marina Duarte**.

### AI-powered attacks

A worrying trend in 2025 is the use of **AI to clone voices and digital identities**.  
Scammers impersonate relatives or crypto exchange employees requesting urgent transfers.

These cases increased **59%** on platforms using stablecoins.

### How to protect yourself

Here are some key recommendations for crypto investors:

- Use wallets with **2FA authentication**
- Never share your seed phrase – **not even with support**
- Always verify exchange reputation
- Be skeptical of “guaranteed quick profits”
- Enable alerts for every transaction
- Keep your devices updated

Security experts emphasize that the **seed phrase** must be stored offline — preferably handwritten in a secure place.  
Losing access to it means **losing all your crypto**, with no recovery possible.

### Regulations are evolving, but challenges remain

Central Bank and financial authorities continue to expand rules for the sector, but technology moves fast.

> “Regulators are adapting — and so are the scammers,” explains blockchain professor **Eduardo Nogueira**.

### The future of digital security

Despite the risks, the market is expected to continue growing.  
New **privacy-focused and audit-ready blockchain solutions** promise to make transactions more reliable.
